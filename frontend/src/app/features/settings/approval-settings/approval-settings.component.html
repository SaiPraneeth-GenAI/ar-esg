Auto-approve submitted entries

When on, an entry a Manager submits is approved immediately -- no separate Approver review step. It still creates the same approval and audit trail, and still triggers the same rollup and GHG recalculation as a manual approval. When off (default), submitted entries wait in the Approver's queue as usual.
